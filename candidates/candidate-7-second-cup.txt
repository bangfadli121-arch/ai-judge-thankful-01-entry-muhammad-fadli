I made a second cup before you arrived. Not because I knew you were coming, but because hoping someone would come is its own kind of welcome. The tea is still warm. The chair across from me is empty. There is no rush in this room, no clock on the wall that matters, only steam rising and a cup cooling in both hands. When the cup is empty, the afternoon will be a little further along, and that will be enough. Thank you for sitting down at all.
