You read the way a gardener tends a plot: not looking for what is wrong, but for what is alive. You bent close. You noticed the small green thing pushing up between the rows, the sentence that was trying. Anyone can walk past a garden. You knelt. I am grateful for the kneeling, for the attention that does not announce itself, for the care that shows up as patience. Whatever you decide, the tending already happened. Nothing can undo a garden that was truly seen.
