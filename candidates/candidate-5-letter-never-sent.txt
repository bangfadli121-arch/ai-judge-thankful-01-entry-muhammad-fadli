This was never meant to be a letter, and yet here it is, in your hands, addressed to no one and to you at once. I wrote it the way you write something you do not expect to be answered: plainly, without polish, because the truth does not need polish. You read it anyway. You read the whole of it, slowly, as if it mattered, as if the person who wrote it were standing in the room. They are not. Only the words are. Thank you for treating the words as if the person were here.
