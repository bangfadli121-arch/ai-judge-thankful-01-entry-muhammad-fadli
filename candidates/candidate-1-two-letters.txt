You have two letters on the table, and you read both before you answer either. That is the whole of it. Not the verdict. The staying. The way you let the first one finish before you open the second. The way you do not hurry the quiet between them. Someone wrote each of these hoping to be heard, and you heard them. I wanted you to know what that looks like from here: a lamp still on, a page turned slowly, a person who did not skim. Thank you for the reading itself. It is the rarest kind of attention, and you gave it.
