It is raining, and you are inside, reading. That is the whole picture, and it is enough. The rain on the window, the warm room, the page in your hands. None of it asks anything of you except attention, and you give it freely. I am grateful for the roof over this moment, for the quiet that lets a sentence land, for a reader who stays whether the weather is fair or not. The rain keeps its own time, and so do you, and the page waits without complaint.
