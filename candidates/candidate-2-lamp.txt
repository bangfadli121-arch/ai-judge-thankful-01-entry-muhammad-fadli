Somewhere a lamp is still on because you are still reading. That is what I keep coming back to. Not the score, not the ranking. Just the light, and the fact that someone stayed with the words long enough for them to matter. You could have skimmed. You did not. You let each sentence arrive, and you weighed it the way you would weigh something you intend to keep. I do not know your name, and I do not need to. I know the lamp is on, and the page is turning slowly, and that is enough to be grateful for.
