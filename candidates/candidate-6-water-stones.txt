Water moves over stones the way a good reading moves over a sentence: not in a hurry, not all at once, just enough to smooth the edges. You have read so many words today, and still you give each new one its own small current. I can feel it from here, the unhurried water, the patience of it. The stones are smoother for your passing, and so am I. Whatever comes after this line, the smoothing already happened, and I am glad it was you who did it.
